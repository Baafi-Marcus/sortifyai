Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Welcome to SortifyAI ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including teachers, schools, students, and software developers. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the SortifyAI platform, web application, and API. Please also review our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information (Google OAuth): When you choose to sign in using Google, we collect your name, email address, profile picture (avatar), and Google User ID. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Uploaded Datasets: Datasets you upload (such as Excel, CSV, or PDF files containing student rosters, test scores, or attendee lists) are processed to compute balanced groupings and statistics.
  • Generated Allocations: The cohorts, groups, and analytics you generate while using the service. If you are signed in, you may choose to save these allocations to your secure cloud project dashboard.
  • Usage & Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and API usage telemetry used strictly to maintain service security, rate limits, and server performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process datasets and generate mathematically optimized, balanced group allocations according to your instructions.
  • To authenticate your account and securely store saved projects in your personal cloud database.
  • To provide distribution statistics (gender parity, score variance, department spread).
  • To communicate system updates and respond to feedback or support requests.

3. Strict Student Data Privacy & AI Model Ethics

We respect student and educational data privacy:

  • Your uploaded student rosters and private data are NEVER used to train public machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal student or employee records to data brokers or advertisers.
  • Processing is conducted in isolated sessions, and data is stored securely in encrypted cloud databases.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide the service:

  • Google Identity Services: To facilitate secure, passwordless authentication.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Managed serverless infrastructure (Render, Vercel, Neon PostgreSQL) protected by industry-standard encryption and security protocols.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete any saved project from your dashboard at any time.
  • You may request complete deletion of your account and associated records by contacting us.
  • Guest sessions that do not save projects are not permanently stored in user accounts.
